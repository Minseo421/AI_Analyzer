- **RQ1:** How are OSS projects adapting contribution governance practices in response to AI-assisted development?
- **RQ2:** To what extent do contributors comply with AI disclosure policies through observable disclosures in OSS pull requests?
- **RQ3:** How do policy visibility and governance characteristics relate to observed AI-use disclosure rates across OSS repositories?

For traceability, the stored main-data measurement is called **detector-observed disclosure presence**. It includes explicit statements that AI was not used; it is neither actual AI-use prevalence nor, without additional policy evidence and human validation, verified policy compliance. The word “adapting” in RQ1 likewise does not make a single policy snapshot evidence of change over time or causation by AI-assisted development.

## RQ2: observable disclosure versus verified compliance

The verified stored repository calculation is:

`observed detector disclosure rate = AI Disclosure Present Count / Eligible PRs Reviewed`

It corresponds to R02's `PRs With Disclosure / Successfully Analysed Eligible PRs`. R01's label `Disclosure Compliance Rate` does not change what the numerator or denominator measures. Both rate representations agree with P01, to their saved precision.

The methodology's intended final calculation instead restricts the denominator to PRs where disclosure was required by the applicable policy, and uses validated evidence to assess fulfillment. In this snapshot:

- All 41,338 `AI Disclosure Required` values are `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; a verified required-policy denominator is unavailable.
- `Disclosure Classification` contains 10,247 `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, 930 `possible_positive`, 99 `possible_negative`, 322 `possible_ambiguous` and 29,740 `None`. These are not a complete set of final human classifications.
- Negative disclosures count as observable disclosure, not evidence that AI was used. An absent disclosure establishes neither absent AI use nor known violation of a conditional policy.
- A binary disclosure match does not establish that policy-specific detail requirements were met. The reported exploratory comparisons about disclosure requirements/detail do not themselves validate every PR's compliance.
- Validation performance on 708 PRs does not fill every main-data policy field, validate all 41,338 PRs, or prove compatible detector versions. It must not be substituted for those checks.

Accordingly, this document maps available RQ2 findings to observable disclosure and explicitly leaves verified compliance unresolved. The final report must apply its C02 definition consistently and qualify stronger wording where the evidence remains incomplete.
